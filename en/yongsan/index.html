Programs

What happens in a session

Sling body balance care, high-frequency body balance care with a licensed beauty device, and a one-to-one format throughout.

Sling-wave care

Sling Body Balance Care

A one-to-one sling bodywork program in which waves are delivered with the feet. The format and intensity are set in consultation and adjusted to your condition that day.

Using a licensed beauty device

High-Frequency Body Balance Care

A care session using a high-frequency device licensed as a beauty device. It is not a medical procedure; the area and duration are set in consultation on the day.

A BALINESS program

details to come

Adjusted to your body that day

One-to-one sessions

Every program runs one-to-one. Even within the same program, intensity and sequence are adjusted to your condition that day.

First visit

If this is your first time

Only one guest is seen at a time. When you arrive, we first ask how your body feels and where it is uncomfortable, then set the intensity and sequence of the session around that. It is closer to an hour of being looked after than an hour of instruction.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be fit or flexible to come?

No. Sessions are one-to-one and adjusted to your condition that day; there is no fitness requirement to start.

What programs are available?

Sling Body Balance Care, T.S.W and High-Frequency Body Balance Care, all one-to-one. High-frequency care uses a device licensed as a beauty device, not a medical procedure.
